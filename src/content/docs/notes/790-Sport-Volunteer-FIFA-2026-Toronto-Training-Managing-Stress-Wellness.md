Stress is natural and can be motivating but too much affects physical
and mental health. Anxiety is a body's response to stress, which is
usually short term while anxiety tends to last longer [^1].

## Effects and Causes of Stress

Effects of stress, triggering the fight or flight response:

- Blood pressure and pulse rises
- Breathing speeds up
- Digestive system slows down
- Muscles become more tense
- Sleepiness decreases due to a heightened state of alertness

Causes examples at a large event:

- Difficult situations and people
- New environments, cultures, language
- Lack of knowledge
- Commute difficulties

## Prevention

- Eat, sleep, and rest well
- Plan ahead and be organized like knowing your schedules, time to
  travel, procedures, and what you need (uniform, accreditation)
- Connect with people

## Managing Stress

If there are challenges:

- Communicate and share with others, earlier communication helps others
  and sharing and reduce your stress
- Ask questions
- Exercise, move your body, relax your way like being in a quiet space
  to help take your mind off of stressors

## Helping Others with Stress

Recognize people under stress be observing mental or physical health
issues.

### Signs of Stress

- Losing temper easily, taking things personally
- Tired
- Struggling with simple tasks
- Working long hours, but not productive
- Not meeting due dates
- Reduced judgment, inhibition
- Frequent sickness
- Heavy breathing, sweating

### Helping

- Show empathy
- Listen
- Confirm that feeling stress normal
- Offer support, resources, make time for others

Ask questions like:

- What's one thing you really enjoyed about yesterday?
- What's something you think could be a challenge today?
