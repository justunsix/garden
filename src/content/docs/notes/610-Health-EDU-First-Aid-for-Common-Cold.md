## About

Common cold has no cure and should improve with treatments recommended
below. Antibiotics are not required.

## Symptoms

- Runny nose
- Cough
- Sore throat

## Treatment

Check for fever to determine with flu or COVID-19 symptoms apply and
antiviral treatment.

Summary of below: rest and fluids, soothe symptoms and seek pain relief,
don't spread disease and seek medical if symptoms worsen or continue

- Get rest
- Drink fluids, hydrate with cold or warm fluids and avoid drugs like
  alcohol, caffeine
- Use a humidifier, breathe in stream from hot water or shower
- Optional medicinal items:
  - Acetaminophen (Tylenol) for pain management
  - Nasal spray, drops, throat lozenges, cough drops
  - Use honey, honey drink

Adjust treatment for children.

Keep hand hygiene by washing hands, keep a clean environment, and avoid
close contact with others and sharing items.

Seek medical care when symptoms do not improve or are more severe.
