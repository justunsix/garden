## Overview of Media Operations and Services

Role includes:

- Welcome media representatives to the venue and the designated media
  zones.
- Provide media representatives with information that assists them in
  performing their work, e.g. program schedules.
- Assist with labelling media-specific positions and placing stickers,
  as well as with distributing bibs, gifts and information among
  accredited media representatives.
- Guide photographers to their positions for the pre-match ceremonies
  and team photos.
- Support media representatives in finding their seats in tribunes and
  pitch side areas.
- Assist with press conferences and interviews.

## Media Operations and Broadcast Services Volunteer Role Specific Training

### Role of Media Operations and Services

Mission: create best working environment media coverage of FIFA events
by facilitating media and broadcast people and promoting the tournament.

#### Services

Support journalists, photographers, and broadcast with:

- Information (in person, media site and app support, FAQs)
- Prepare and manage media areas
- Media logistics (ticketing and access control of media spaces,
  parking/shuttle, bibs, seating, networking, camera services)
- Their work in media events, coverage (press conferences, interviews)

#### Groups of People Media Operations Works with

- FIFA Media Operations team, communications, media partnerships and
  relations, host broadcast
- Media:
  - Rights holders
  - Non-rights holders - have limited filming rights
  - Written press and photographers

### Media facilities and operations

- Stadium Media Centre - for accredited media
  - Information, items (bibs, locker, ticket issues), food
- Media tribune - in stadium accessible work area
- Pitch positions
  - Photo positions
  - Broadcast, media partners
- Mixed Zone / I-Zone - accredited media interview teams
- Press conference
- Broadcast compound - technology and working area for host broadcast
  and media partners
- International broadcast centre
- Training centres

## Toronto Stadium Logistics and Schedules

- Encourage public transportation, especially for fans
- Accessibility: app for ASL, hearing disabilities, wheelchairs
  available

### Media Centre

- Reserved seating not available except for agencies
- Amenities: tables, work areas, camera services, Toronto information
  desk, cafe, network and WIFI
- Media briefings: photographer guidelines, positions and flow
- Schedule: team photo opportunity during transport and opening
  ceremonies, press conferences after games, mixed zone for interviews

#### Media operations and technology services

- Registration including gifts, photo bibs
- Media transport
- Ticketing
- Technology And app troubleshooting
- Multi-lingual languages services by staff and volunteers, most common
  were English, French, Spanish, Arabic
- Parking logistics
- Lockers
- Broadcast And Press Media Services
- Photographer Tags And Remote Cameras
- Lost and found

### Tribunes and Photo positions

Power, tables, and water for hydration

Photo positions at tribunes and on pitch (100 pitch positions)
