## Needle Usage

Suggestion for safety and health, discard needles after 1-2 times
personal use.

Maintenance schedule for minor pains can be 1-2 times per week. Check
references for treatment plans [^1].

Place needle in guide tube, tap quickly into the skin until the top of
needle is below guide tube. Expect some pain during the tapping. Prefer
quicker tapping to reduce pain. If desired, can twist needle a bit in,
but avoid veins.

For using the guide tube, use fingers to guide needle into tube.
Generally body areas regularly exposed to the sun will be less sensitive
than areas not regularly exposed. For example, top of foot/hand is less
sensitive than bottom of foot/hand.
