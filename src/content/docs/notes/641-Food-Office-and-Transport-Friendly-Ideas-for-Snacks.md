title: Office And Transport Friendly Ideas For Snacks
---

Ideas for healthy food and drinks as snacks that taste good in an office
environment or are easy to transport and eat on the go.

## Needs

Items must are easy to store and transport (lightweight). They have no
smell and resistant to spoiling. Optionally, some items can go with hot
water, cold water, and refrigeration if available.

## Food Ideas

- crackers and dry foods, protein/granola bars, cookies, trail mix and
  dried foods, nut (but be mindful of possible allergies)
- Fridge: hummus, cheese
- Fruits: apples, pears, clementines, bananas

## Drink Ideas

- Powdered drinks like hot chocolate, tea, coffee/tea packets, protein
  shakes
