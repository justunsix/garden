Removing a bathroom sink stopper that is twist off or held by a plunger
rod [^1].

Try first to remove the stopper by hand with lifting and twisting. If
that does not follow the next steps:

- Clear up the bottom of the sink
- Find the horizontal plunger rod (metal rod) with a metal clip holding
  the stopper in place
- Remove the pivot nut attached to the sink drain. Before unscrewing,
  put a bowl underneath the pivot nut area to catch any water
- Remove the nut and rod. Now remove the stopper
- Clean:
  - Sink stopper
  - Pull, clean sink by hand or drain cleaning tools, be careful when
    putting cleaning objects down the drain
  - Run drain under warm/hot water
  - Use vinegar or detergent
  - Soak the stopper in a soap and water
  - Optional: use drain cleaning liquid
- Reassemble the pieces again
