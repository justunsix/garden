Ideas for holiday celebrations for organizations

- Superlatives/Awards - An award ceremony that celebrates each person's
  unique contributions to the team. Example: Most likely to…win The
  Great Bake-Off
- Secret Meme Santa - Everyone is randomly assigned a team member in
  advance of the gathering, who they will create/modify a meme for. The
  organizer will compile memes and share them with everyone at the
  gathering.
- Skribbl.io - online drawing and guessing game, can customize clues
- Drawbattle - online team drawing game. Similar to Skribbl, with people
  split into two teams to determine which team can draw and guess words
  the fastest
- Scavenger Hunt - Have each person seek out specific items at a
  specified location. When everyone returns with their object, ask
  people to share their item visually for the judges to score. Use clues
  that allow participants get creative like, "One thing I can't live
  without" or "Something that makes me laugh."
- Kahoot - online game learning platform that can be adapted to generate
  custom multiple-choice quizzes, such as a trivia contest.
- Jeopardy - custom trivia for your team or find template on any
  subject. Play in teams or individuals.
- Name That Tune / person / thing - People identify the thing based on
  clues (sounds, pictures, stories, quotations)
- Jack in the Box - online game
