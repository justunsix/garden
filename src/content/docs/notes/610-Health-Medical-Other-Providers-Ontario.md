title: Other Health Care Providers for Ontario
---

These organizations are not a replacement for public funded primary
healthcare services. Their use case can be to supplement existing
services with better accessibility and care coordination and provide
specific services for a fee, for example prescriptions, assessments, and
immunizations.

- [Care& - Nurse Practitioner Led Primary Care](https://careand.ca/)
  including lab services and family health
- [Felix Health](https://www.felixforyou.ca/about/) - healthcare
  platform for on demand treatment of variety of health issues
- [Maple - Online Doctor Appointments, Consultations, and Prescriptions
  in Canada, 24/7](https://www.getmaple.ca/)
- [Private Family Healthcare - Harrison
  Healthcare](https://harrisonhealthcare.ca/overview/) - personal and
  family healthcare service
- [Rocket Doctor: Online Doctors in Canada](https://rocketdoctor.ca/) -
  Primary and Urgent Care
