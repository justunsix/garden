## Define Accessibility and Importance

- Remove barriers for people with disabilities through:
  - Physical access
  - Information access
  - Attitudes
  - Opportunities like using technology
- Benefits to everyone, 15.5% (1/7 people) in Ontario have disabilities
- Range of disabilities:
  - Mobility, Vision, Hearing, Intellectual/developmental,
    speech/language/communications, mental health
- Goals
  - Create great experiences (volunteer with and for people)
  - Make difference in attitudes
  - Ontario Legal compliance
    - Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA)
    - Ontario Human Rights Code
    - Code and laws, higher level of accessibility comes first in a
      conflict

## Communication with and about people with disabilities - "Put the Person First"

- See the person as a whole, not just disability - all people are
  capable
  - Example: people is author, mentor, athlete, wheelchair user, sister,
    volunteer, mother - person is all things
- Use person first language examples:
  - Person with a disability
  - Person with visual impairment
  - Person with speech impairment
  - Athlete with disability
  - Accessible washroom
  - Accessible parking
  - Has polio

## Assist people with disability - "Just ask, Just Listen"

- Just ask
  - Can I help you
  - How can I help
    - Ask what kind of help they want
    - Take initiative for situations that might come up
    - Repeat or try different approaches
- Just listen
  - Can you repeat that
  - Are you saying
