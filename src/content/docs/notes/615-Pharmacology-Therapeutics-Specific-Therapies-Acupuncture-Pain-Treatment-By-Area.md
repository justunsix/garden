title: Acupuncture Pain Treatment By Area
---

This article only describes notes on treatments or simplified treatment.
See references for professional treatment instructions [^1] [^2] [^3].

## General Pain

Applicable to most areas:

- Elbow crease close to exterior of body

- Large Intestine LI 4 Hegu - Hand between thumb and index top finger
  bones

- Apply needle at or at either sides of pain site. For example, wrist
  pain, use the above points described (elbow crease, hand).

## Tennis and Golf Elbow

- Around pain muscle area
- In line across elbow area

## Gastrointestinal (GI) related

- Foot:
  - Stomach ST44
  - Liver LR2
  - Some points between toes
- Spleen 6
  - Inside of ankle + 3 fingers
- Stomach ST36
  - Below knee, indentation below knee cap

## Nose, Allergies

- Left, right base of outer part of nose

## Knee, Lower Back

Notes on bursitis treatment in those areas:

- Bursae area
- Leg alley, nerve endings beginning below bottom to knee crease
- Knee crease, both sides
- Mid back line up to pelvic crest line, then 1 needle on either side of
  mid back line. Diagram from Joseph Wong describes placement on lower
  back [^4].

## Eyes, Facial Relaxation

- Inner points at end of eye brows

## Neck pain

- Back of head, lower area of neck, occipital - can help with cold
  prevention and immune system
