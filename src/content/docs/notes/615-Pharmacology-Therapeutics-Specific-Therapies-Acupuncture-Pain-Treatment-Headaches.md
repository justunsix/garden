## Treatment

Head pain treatment [^1] [^2].

### Simplified Treatment

Follow instructions for general pain described at [Acupuncture Pain
Treatment By
Area](/garden/notes/615-pharmacology-therapeutics-specific-therapies-acupuncture-pain-treatment-by-area) -
[Acupuncture Pain Treatment By
Area](id:20a25a5a-5759-487b-8520-a810fc37345c)

- Elbow crease close to exterior of body
- Hand between thumb and index top finger bones
- 1-3 or more points at top of head meridian, can be close to pain area

### Treatment per Manual of Neuro-Anatomical Acupuncture

Summary of treatment for chronic tension headache [^3] pages 55-60:

Tension headache is "caused by muscle contraction … in response to
stress, anxiety, depression fatigue, emotional conflicts, or repressed
hostility". "Muscles become ischemic and painful."

Acupuncture treatment of tension headache:

- GB20, LI 18, GB 21
- GB 14
- GV 18
- Tai Yang
- SI 15
- GV 16

Expect pain to be asymptomatic 20-30mins after treatment. Maintenance is
2 times weekly for 3-4 weeks.
