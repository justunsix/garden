## Methodology from Digital Micro-Learning Sprint: Agile 2

Session led by David Tallan, Anna Boyden

How do you do it?

- Fail quickly / plan for it
- To do lists / Kanban (backlog, in progress, in review, done), moved
  away from sprints
- Issues could take between 1 day to years depending on the issue. All
  bugs, issues, features are all in the backlog

"Be a Learner" - How did you / your team balance time to learn
vs. development. Due to the speed of agile, teams encounter new things
often and the learning backlog for individuals can be as large as the
development things to work on :-)

- Make a regular time in your day/week to do the learning
- Prioritize items in backlog that will give you the most *learning*
  rather than just value

Important skills?

- Communication and effective interaction, consensus building

Tool to use?

- JIRA
- GitHub
- Asana
- Microsoft Teams, Office 365
  - Groups by project
  - Wiki

Important relationships?

- Development and business team - if they are together, need a good
  dev-product manager

Co-design with Ontario citizens?

- Get feedback with consultation regularly with users or representative
  stakeholders and decision makers, Plan Do Check Act. Build trust with
  stakeholders
